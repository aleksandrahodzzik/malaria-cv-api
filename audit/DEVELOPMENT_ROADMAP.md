# Development roadmap — canonical

Текущий рекомендуемый путь: стратегия A, research-only cell API. Переход к B/C
или D является отдельным gated decision.

- 0–7 дней: model/intended-purpose decisions, claims/security triage, smoke
  design.
- 8–30 дней: registry/manifest, Datasheet/Model Card, evaluation/calibration,
  lock и metrics.
- 31–60 дней: external cohort, QC/OOD, patient protocol, T2/T3, auth/staging.
- 61–90 дней: silent pilot, human factors, resilience, risk/change control.
- 3–6 месяцев: multi-site validation, conditional slide/counting R&D,
  regulatory/QMS and controlled release/PMS.

Deliverables, owner, dependencies, measurable exits, risks and effort:
`phase19/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.csv`.
