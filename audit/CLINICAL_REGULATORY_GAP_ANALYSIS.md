# Clinical and regulatory gap analysis

Это условный gap analysis, не юридическое заключение.

## Current use

Research/educational classification of a pre-cropped cell. No patient
diagnosis, screening, triage, treatment recommendation or autonomous action.

## Clinical gaps

- intended population/user/setting/acquisition protocol undefined;
- no detector/slide aggregation/reference workflow;
- no patient-level external/prospective evidence;
- no calibrated uncertainty/QC/OOD reject;
- no validated human review, override or incident workflow.

## Potential frameworks

Exact intended purpose and market determine applicability of EU IVDR/MDR, EU
AI Act, GDPR, FDA/IMDRF SaMD and national rules. Potential standards include
ISO 13485:2016, ISO 14971:2019, IEC 62304:2006+A1:2015,
IEC 62366-1:2015+A1:2020, IEC 81001-5-1:2021 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022.

Official sources and access dates are recorded in `SOURCES.md`; no presence of
Docker/tests/checklists is treated as compliance.

## Required before clinical path

Controlled intended purpose, QMS/risk/software/usability/cyber lifecycle,
performance evaluation, human factors, change control, release traceability,
post-market/incident monitoring and market-specific professional review.
